English Grammar

Learn English grammar from the basics with simple rules, structures, examples and common mistakes.

← English Learning Hub
01

Parts of Speech

Words are classified according to their function in a sentence.
Noun • Pronoun • Verb • Adjective • Adverb • Preposition • Conjunction • Interjection
Example:
Rahim reads a good book.
02

Article

Use a before a consonant sound and an before a vowel sound. Use the for a specific person or thing.
a + consonant sound
an + vowel sound
the + specific noun
Example:
He is a student.
She ate an apple.
03

Tense

Tense shows the time of an action or state.
Present • Past • Future
Example:
I study English.
I studied English.
I will study English.
04

Subject & Verb

The verb must agree with its subject in number and person.
Subject + Verb + Object
Example:
He plays football.
They play football.
Common mistake: He play football. ✗
05

Preposition

Prepositions show relationships such as place, time and direction.
in • on • at • by • with • from • to
Example:
The book is on the table.
06

Voice

Voice shows whether the subject performs an action or receives it.
Active → Subject + Verb + Object
Passive → Object + be + V3
Example:
Active: Rahim writes a letter.
Passive: A letter is written by Rahim.
07

Narration

Narration changes direct speech into reported speech while keeping the original meaning.
Direct Speech → Reported Speech
Example:
Direct: He said, “I am happy.”
Reported: He said that he was happy.
08

Right Form of Verb

Choose the correct form of the verb according to tense, subject and sentence structure.
Subject + appropriate verb form
Example:
He goes to school every day.
09

Changing Sentence

A sentence can be transformed into another form while preserving its basic meaning.
Affirmative ↔ Negative
Assertive ↔ Interrogative
Exclamatory ↔ Assertive
Example:
He is honest.
He is not dishonest.
10

Punctuation & Capitalization

Correct punctuation and capitalization make writing clear and easy to understand.
. , ? ! : ; " "
Example:
My name is Rahim. I live in Bangladesh.
